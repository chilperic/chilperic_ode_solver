Newton and validation settings

The browser uses finite-difference Jacobians, partial-pivot Gaussian elimination, and residual-decreasing backtracking. Convergence is not claimed unless the final residual meets the requested tolerance.

Parameter scan settings

These are sequential parameter scans using the previous converged root as the next initial guess. They are not pseudo-arclength continuation and do not prove folds or Hopf bifurcations. Any crossing or turning marker is a grid-dependent candidate only.
