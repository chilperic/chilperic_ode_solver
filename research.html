Boundary rules

Teaching modelsStay in the Model Atlas and labs. They can expose editable equations and exports.
Published or thesis-derived researchCan be described more openly, with reduced simulations and reproducibility links where safe.
Unpublished plant workShows narrative, selected figures and protected surrogates only. No code, parameter files, notebooks, raw data, exports or download links.
