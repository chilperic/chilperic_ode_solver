C3 / intermediate / C4 display definitions
These are illustrative allocation labels, not inferred species or validated photosynthetic categories. C3-like: mesophyll Rubisco fraction ≥ 0.9. C4-like: mesophyll ≤ 0.1, PEPC ≥ 0.1, sheath Rubisco ≥ 0.1. Intermediate-like: all three fractions ≥ 0.05, excluding those endpoints. Remaining allocations are unclassified. These fixed thresholds affect display counts only, never selection or reproduction; infeasible individuals are counted by allocation too.
Plane markers identify reference allocations C3 (0,1,0), intermediate (0.2,0.5,0.3), C4 (0.4,0,0.6). A C3→intermediate→C4 transition is not forced. The model has no explicit C2 photorespiratory shuttle. Life-cycle replay shows the recorded population, expected selection weights, then its actual offspring and ancestry. Selection-marker area encodes parent probability; this is not an individual survival event. At most 80 offspring have ancestry links drawn to keep the plot readable; full ancestry is exported. Smooth-path mode interpolates means only. Playback speed does not alter the simulation.
Connection to the attached v13 specification
Sections 11–13 separate physiological ledgers, landscape mapping and finite-population inheritance. This workspace implements a reduced three-trait experiment: two allocation coordinates and stomatal conductance. The graphical abstract illustrates hypotheses; C3 retention, intermediates and C4-like outcomes must emerge from the calculated model, not from pre-drawn peaks.
The supplied heat manuscript motivates coupled energy and water balances. This repair retains the existing steady-state leaf solver; it does not implement the complete v13 dynamic soil, anatomical, hydraulic and biochemical system. Reproductive calibration and the benchmark suite remain necessary before evolutionary prediction.
Equations and interpretation
C=∑AkΔtk,W=∑EkΔtk
A in µmol/m²/s is converted to mol before integrating over seconds; water is mol/m². Heat exposure H = Σ max(0, max(Tmes,Tbs) − Tthreshold) Δt in K·h. Temperature cutoff, minimum carbon and solver feasibility remain separate constraints.
Normalized log-performance r = wC C/Cref − wW W/Wref − wH H/Href. In population evolution, b = exp(r); optionally v = logistic(γ₀ + γ₁r). Parent probabilities are proportional to vb, calculated in log space without another exponential tilt. Viability is an expected parent-contribution factor; explicit individual survival and variable population size are not simulated.
Two allocation breeding values map through softmax(a₁,a₂,0); a third maps to gs through a bounded logistic. Mutation and segregation occur on the latent scale, without hard clipping in the biological model. Sexual offspring receive the parental mean plus segregation and mutation; asexual offspring inherit one parent plus mutation. Correlated noise uses a positive-definite equicorrelation matrix. Effective population size is not inferred from census size.
The grid includes exact allocation boundaries. Continuous softmax searches/populations approach boundaries but do not reach exact zero. A C3 or C4 species identity must not be inferred from a threshold chosen after inspecting results.
NSGA-II uses nondominated sorting, crowding distance, binary tournaments, arithmetic crossover and Gaussian variation. CMA-ES reuses the uploaded FokoLab numerical core; covariance adaptation is not biological genetic covariance. Neither search guarantees a global optimum or a complete Pareto front.
Unavailable in this implementation: dynamic soil replay, C2 glycine shuttling, calibrated risk and infrastructure costs, resource-budget evolution, locus-specific genetics, variable demography, a tested breeder-equation benchmark and SLiM. These remain distinct extensions; they are not silently represented by optimizer iterations. A separate diploid Kimura origin–fixation workspace now provides rare-mutation paths, optimization comparators, Sobol, Morris and PCA.
The earlier haploid experiment remains accessible for comparison. Its clipped fraction mutations and single assimilation score do not implement the quantitative-genetic specification in working document v13.
