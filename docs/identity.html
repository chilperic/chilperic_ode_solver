Three visual roles remain separate

Where you are

The page accent identifies its scientific area. It stays consistent across navigation, headings and controls.

What happened

Run status uses explicit words and symbols. A successful calculation does not become a claim of biological validity.

What the data say

Scientific curves retain their own series identities, line styles and units. Changing a page accent does not change the quantities being plotted.

The book’s own seven parts
