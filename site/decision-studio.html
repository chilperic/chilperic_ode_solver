Method and limitations
Finite-set dominance and crowding are deterministic. A sampled nondominated archive is not a complete global front. Knee preferences and confidence bands are not invented. Pareto candidate correlations are descriptive, not independent-replicate hypothesis tests.
Optimizer evaluations, generations and accepted events remain separate clocks. Population outcomes are not equivalent to a numerical optimum.
