Learn a simulator surrogate

Train an interpretable regression on a conditional slice of your own model. Then test it at untouched parameter values.

Twelve disjoint parameter settings test the fit. Two further points challenge behavior 10% beyond the range. All other parameters remain nominal.

What “AI” means here

Supervised polynomial regression with train-only scaling and a fixed ridge penalty. No neural network or language model is used. The comparison reports a constant baseline and a linear baseline.

Choose degree before looking at the test score. Repeated tuning on the same holdout requires a new independent evaluation.
