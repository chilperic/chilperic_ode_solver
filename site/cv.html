Research areas and methods

  • Systems biology and mechanistic modeling.
  • Hepatic metabolism, lipid dynamics and fatty-acid synthesis.
  • Plant physiology, photosynthetic adaptation and climate-response modeling.
  • Quantitative immunology and T-cell proliferation dynamics.
  • ODE, DAE, stochastic, constraint-based and differentiable metabolic models.
  • Parameter estimation, calibration, sensitivity and uncertainty analysis.

Scientific computing stack

  • Languages: Python, Julia, R, MATLAB, JavaScript.
  • Optimization: CasADi, IPOPT, CMA-ES, JuMP, Pyomo, MEALPY.
  • Scientific computing: SciPy, NumPy, CVODE, SUNDIALS, lmfit.
  • Visualization: pandas, matplotlib, ggplot2, Plotly, Bokeh.
  • Tool development: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and browser-based interfaces.

Experience distilled for Foko Lab

  • Postdoctoral research: first-principles thermo-hydraulic-biochemical modeling framework for photosynthetic adaptation across the C3 to C4 continuum.
  • PhD research: nonlinear and stochastic models of hepatic fatty-acid metabolism, enzyme kinetics, differentiable constraint-based metabolic modeling and bistability.
  • Earlier research: stochastic and deterministic models for T-cell proliferation and CFSE-type cell-division data.
  • Scientific software: browser-native modeling tools, reproducible repositories, simulation examples, model export and documentation-driven workflows.
