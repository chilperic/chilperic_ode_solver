Methods · interpretation

Optimisation finds peaks. Evolution must find paths.

FokoLab keeps numerical search and biological evolution visually close but conceptually separate. That distinction is essential for interpreting the C3–C4 research programme.

Two questions

“What performs well?” is not “what can evolve?”

The first is an optimisation/landscape problem. The second adds population genetics, constraints, history and stochasticity.

Landscape & optimisation

  • Locate high-performing regions
  • Map trade-offs and feasibility boundaries
  • Compare objectives and constraints
  • Use CMA-ES, grids or Pareto methods as search tools

Biological evolution

  • Start from plausible ancestral populations
  • Represent heritable variation and accessible mutations
  • Apply reproduction, selection and drift
  • Compare replicated trajectories across environmental histories
