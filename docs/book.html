        One book. Three ways to work.

        Read the scientific source
        439 physical page positions, with four private planning pages omitted; scientific explanations and technical figures, 27 worked chapter openings, 54 practice problems, and appendices A–E are retained.
        Work a chapter
        Open its first teaching section, attempt its two practice problems, then consult the worked solutions in Appendix A. Research investigations have separate diagnostic guidance in Appendix B.
        Transfer to an experiment
        Open a related FokoLab tool and retain an experiment as programme evidence. Browser companions are adaptations, not claims of a complete reproduction of the chapter.
        Original teaching and notebook code
        The PDF refers to teaching/examples and studios/learner, studios/solutions and studios/executed. Their full source archive is not included: only the PDF was supplied. No placeholder download is presented as that archive.

        The web reader supplies navigation and search, not a complete accessible HTML edition. Section 1.6 is separately available as a reflowable source excerpt.
