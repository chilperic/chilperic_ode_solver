Scientific plots

Local sensitivity

Identifies which tested traits most strongly affect the selected model output near the reference state. It is local evidence, not global importance.

Global sensitivity

Separates direct first-order effects from total effects that include interactions under the stated parameter distributions.

Parameter interactions

Shows that model behaviour depends on combinations of traits rather than a single controlling parameter.

Carbon-isotope diagnostic

One diagnostic used to interpret motion across C3-like and C4-like physiological regimes. It is not used alone to classify an evolutionary state.

Performance landscape

Maps high- and low-performing regions of a selected trait subspace. Landscape maxima are candidate physiological optima, not proofs of evolutionary endpoints.

Ecological context

Contextual figure linking the modelling problem to observed C4 occurrence. Observational distributions constrain interpretation but do not directly calibrate every model parameter.

Primary scientific questions

  • Starting from plausible C3-like populations, which trait changes are physiologically viable and evolutionarily accessible?
  • Which combinations of anatomy, carbon-concentrating mechanisms, hydraulics, thermal regulation and resource allocation create accessible C3–C4 intermediate or C4-like phenotypes?
  • How do climate history, drought, temperature, water temperature, uncertainty, mutation, inheritance, selection and drift change those routes?
  • Which apparent optima disappear once energetic, protein/nitrogen, hydraulic and structural costs are included?

How I separate the science

Physiology determines whether a phenotype is physically and biologically admissible. Landscape analysis maps performance, sensitivity, uncertainty and trade-offs. Evolution requires heritable variation, reproduction, selection and drift. Numerical optimizers help explore landscapes but are not biological generations.

C2 is not the focus of the project. It is one possible intermediate photorespiratory mechanism when required by the biology. The target is a scientifically defensible mechanism spanning C3, C3–C4 intermediates and C4.
