What does selection favor?

The default objective rewards daily net carbon gain, then subtracts weighted water use and heat exposure. Each quantity is divided by its declared reference scale. Higher scores increase reproductive success; drift can still fix lower-scoring mutations. C4 receives no categorical bonus.

Water and heat weights are scenario choices. Heat exposure is accumulated temperature above 35 °C; 50 °C is a hard thermal limit. Infeasible physiological states cannot reproduce. Search iterations are not evolutionary generations.

Scientific meaning, limits and references

GDC (glycine decarboxylase) releases photorespiratory CO₂. The exploratory extension moves part of this release into the bundle sheath, where Rubisco can refix it. It shares the existing whole-leaf energy budget. It does not include calibrated nitrogen-shuttle or transport costs, or evolving anatomy; C2 benefits should therefore be interpreted as an upper-bound scenario.

Labels are operational diagnostics, not species identifications or experimentally fitted boundaries. With mesophyll and sheath Rubisco present, 50% GDC localization gives the incipient category and 100% gives the C2 categories at the assumed 50% transport efficiency. The PEPC-free case is Type I-like; PEPC-containing cases are Type II-like. C4-like requires mesophyll allocation ≤10%, with PEPC and sheath each ≥10%. C3-like requires mesophyll allocation ≥90%. These labels never enter selection. Proto-Kranz anatomy is not represented.

Which traits influence the output?

Sobol and Morris evaluate the physiological mapping over declared independent ranges. They do not estimate sensitivity of an evolutionary endpoint. PCA describes observed trait variation and cannot identify causal importance.

Morris: number of 4-level trajectories. Sobol: number of independent A/B base pairs. PCA uses completed experiment records.

Sensitivity varies the listed factors while keeping this recycling setting fixed. PCA describes only the three recorded allocation/conductance coordinates; it does not measure GDC variation.
