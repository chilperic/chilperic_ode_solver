Publication boundary

Public evidence without publishing unfinished science.

For unpublished work, the public interface should show the question, simulation outputs, plots, uncertainty status and collaboration. It should not expose the complete equations, calibration files or reconstructable parameterisation.

Visible publicly

  • Simulation animations and archived result replays
  • Scientific figures, sensitivity and trade-off analyses
  • Research questions, model classes and limitations
  • Parameter evidence categories and uncertainty labels
  • Collaborators, publications and model provenance

Protected while unpublished

  • Complete scientific equations and source implementation
  • Detailed calibration datasets and parameter registry
  • Unpublished mechanistic hypotheses and development notes
  • Private notebooks, raw results and research administration
  • Anything sufficient to reconstruct the unpublished solver

Research method

Model → diagnose → challenge → revise.

The platform is designed around scientific interrogation rather than one-click answers. Solver convergence, parameter uncertainty and model limitations remain visible instead of being collapsed into a single score.
