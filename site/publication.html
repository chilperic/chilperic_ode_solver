Three distinct model sources

New teaching reductions: seasonal plant resources, two-trait evolutionary fitness, the leaf thermal experiment, generation-resolved cell examples and the mathematical demonstrations. Equations, illustrative parameter choices and limits are available in each laboratory.

Retained public research reductions: the 18-state FADNS and four-state fatty-acid models, plus original research-inspired agent and connected-workflow examples. Their presence does not make them complete calibrated research implementations.

Unpublished plant research: interactive simulations and selected results are available online. Full source and calibration datasets are not included in public downloads.

Code and source attribution

Existing source acknowledgements, creator pages and collaborator attribution are retained. References to notebooks in the book describe the book’s source ecosystem; they do not assert that those notebook files are bundled in this website.

The exact source/public PDF hashes are recorded in the publication record. A hash verifies file identity, not scientific validity or distribution rights.
