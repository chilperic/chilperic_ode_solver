Platform implementation & visual explanation

FokoLab is developed by Dr. Chilperic Armel Foko Kuate with AI-assisted implementation and testing. Research collaborators are not automatically authors of the browser implementation. The new mathematical explanations draw inspiration from 3Blue1Brown and Seeing Theory; no branding, video, code or artwork is copied.

Method references, transformations, source configurations and known limitations accompany each analysis. Numerical checks do not establish biological calibration or clinical utility.
