Publication and research boundaries

The public website includes a derivative of Scientific Mastery V6.17 Design Revision. The scientific chapters, section links, worked practice destinations and appendices keep the same physical page numbers.

What is distributed

Physical source pages 6–9 contain private author-planning material and are replaced by explicit omission notices. The full author PDF is retained, unchanged, only in the complete package’s private-reference/ folder. The public-site ZIP and Pages workflow do not distribute that folder or the original rollback archive.

Three distinct model sources

New teaching reductions: seasonal plant resources, two-trait evolutionary fitness, the leaf thermal experiment, generation-resolved cell examples and the mathematical demonstrations. Equations, illustrative parameter choices and limits are available in each laboratory.

Retained public research reductions: the 18-state FADNS and four-state fatty-acid models, plus original research-inspired agent and connected-workflow examples. Their presence does not make them complete calibrated research implementations.

Protected or unavailable full research: no inaccessible hosted source, private calibration data, protected C3–C4 implementation or unpublished full research code was reconstructed and certified by this release.

Code and source attribution

Existing source acknowledgements, creator pages and collaborator attribution are retained. References to notebooks in the book describe the book’s source ecosystem; they do not assert that those notebook files are bundled in this website. No missing notebook has been fabricated.

The exact source/public PDF hashes are recorded in the publication record. A hash verifies file identity, not scientific validity or distribution rights.
