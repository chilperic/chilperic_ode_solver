What the animation means

The motion is presentation of archived scientific outputs. It does not rerun the unpublished mechanistic model in the browser and is not labelled as a reconstructed natural lineage.

Interpretation

Search paths and evolutionary paths are different objects.

Numerical optimisation can map high-performing regions efficiently, but biological evolution additionally requires inheritance, heritable variation, reproduction, selection, drift and accessible intermediate states.
