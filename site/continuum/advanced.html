What does selection favor?

The default objective maximizes time-weighted mean net CO₂ assimilation over the whole forcing cycle, including dark rows. Its units are µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹; a declared reference converts it to a dimensionless score. Illuminated-hours and weighted trade-off objectives are separate choices. Higher scores increase reproductive success; drift can still fix lower-scoring mutations. C4 receives no categorical bonus.

Water and heat weights are scenario choices. Heat exposure is accumulated temperature above 35 °C; 50 °C is a hard thermal limit. Infeasible physiological states cannot reproduce. Search iterations are not evolutionary generations.

Classification thresholds and sensitivity

These are exploratory display rules, not universal species boundaries. Allocation, enzyme activity, anatomy and gas exchange jointly distinguish photosynthetic types. Changing a label threshold never changes physiology, fitness or evolutionary transitions.

The C3-like label requires the mesophyll allocation floor, pump throughput no higher than the small-pump threshold, and recycling below the strong-recycling threshold. C4-like requires low mesophyll allocation, at least 10% in each of the other pools, and an active pump. Remaining allocations with recycling are separated into incipient, Type I-like (small pump), and Type II-like (larger pump).

Activity ratios compare daylight PEPC pump or relocated photorespiratory CO₂ to gross Rubisco carboxylation. They are model throughput diagnostics; they are not measured fractions of carbon refixed. Grey means another feasible allocation. Hatching marks infeasible or unresolved samples.

Which traits influence the output?

Sobol and Morris evaluate the physiological mapping over declared independent ranges. They do not estimate sensitivity of an evolutionary endpoint. PCA describes observed trait variation and cannot identify causal importance.

Morris: number of 4-level trajectories. Sobol: number of independent A/B base pairs. PCA uses completed experiment records.

Sensitivity varies the listed factors while keeping this recycling setting fixed. PCA describes only the three recorded allocation/conductance coordinates; it does not measure GDC variation.
