What you can do

  • Simulate dynamical systems and inspect numerical diagnostics.
  • Compare parameter choices with fitting, sensitivity and optimization tools.
  • Analyze data and prepare labelled, exportable figures.
  • Save experiments locally and download records for backup or transfer.
Scope and limitations

Model scope and calibration vary by laboratory. Numerical checks do not establish biological validity. Review the assumptions and evidence for the selected model before using its predictions.

Saved experiments are stored in this browser; download a backup before switching devices.
