Scientific scope and numerical evidence

A rendered plot is not a validation certificate. Model assumptions, numerical execution, observation semantics and empirical adequacy are separate claims.

Original numerical tools are retained

Use the method-specific diagnostics in the optimization, sensitivity, fitting, statistics and other original laboratories. Their original source implementations have not been replaced with smaller demonstrations.

New biological experiments

Plant resources: finite carbon, water and nitrogen ledgers; explicit precipitation, irrigation, tissue turnover, mineralization, leaching, single-season senescence and seed-bank carry-over. Net carbon production is a leaf-carbon-dependent phenomenological response. Root hydraulics, stomatal optimization, leaf area dynamics and calibrated yield prediction are not implemented.

Leaf physiology: a fixed-kinetics C3 Rubisco/light limitation calculation, and a separate dynamic heat-balance experiment with prescribed transpiration. These are not a coupled C3/C4/CAM research physiology model.

Adaptation: a newly authored finite-season fitness proxy and weak-mutation Moran substitutions. The season samples a stated window of the environmental cycle. C3/intermediate/C4 shading is an illustrative trait legend, not an inferred boundary or evidence of an evolutionary pathway.

T cells: a finite-generation ODE teaching model. Original agent and connected-research workflows remain available. Neither is a replacement for full Smith–Martin, Cyton or patient-data inference.

Fatty-acid interpretation remains qualified

The original 18-state FADNS and four-pool fatty-acid equations are unchanged. Total FAS occupancy and numerical reference comparisons can test those equations’ implementation. They do not validate a calibrated mechanism or establish bistability.

The relationship between the source research bistability claim and a browser uniqueness argument is not resolved here. The original full equations, parameter assumptions, admissible domain and independent analysis would have to be compared. No equation or tolerance was changed merely to obtain an expected number of steady states.

How to read the tests

Analytic limits, conserved quantities, independently executed Python calculations and known-answer core probes are stronger than import-only checks. Nevertheless, their scope is bounded. Historical test totals are retained only as historical documents in the author package and do not certify this release.
