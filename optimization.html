Search method and evidence settings

The browser reports the best evaluated candidate under a quadratic-penalty search and then checks constraints independently. A feasible candidate is not a certified local optimum; a heuristic or multi-start result is not a proof of global optimality. KKT conditions, convexity, uniqueness, and robustness are not inferred unless separately established outside this reference lab.
