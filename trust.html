How to interpret a result

What Foko Lab computed — and where the claim stops.

Every capability carries a label that tells you whether the result was computed in the browser, produced by a bounded method, prepared for an external tool, or not offered.

Known limitations

  • Local finite differences are local and step-dependent; discontinuities, event thresholds and poor scaling can invalidate derivative interpretations.
  • Initial conditions affect trajectories but are not treated as sensitivity factors in this release.

  • Two-parameter response surfaces fix all remaining parameters nominal and are not variance decompositions.
  • Time-resolved Jansen estimates reuse the finite seeded global design; low-variance time points may be unresolved.
  • Histogram mutual information and RBF-HSIC are bounded-sample permutation screening diagnostics, not variance fractions or causal measures.
  • The normalized Morris trajectory view displays only a bounded subset of the computed designs when many trajectories are requested; all trajectories still contribute to the statistics.
  • State-resolved first and total indices apply the selected scalar metric to each state using the same finite seeded Jansen design; they are not independent replications.
  • The optional two-parameter response surface is available in Local and Global variance modes but remains an OFAT-style bounded grid with all other parameters fixed at nominal values.

  • A finite seeded realization does not establish mutation-selection equilibrium or inevitable convergence to the global optimum.

Known limitations
  • The Gaussian-process interval is conditional on the RBF kernel and user-selected hyperparameters.
  • The random-feature surrogate is not deep-network training and reports no predictive uncertainty.
  • All displayed errors are in-sample; deployment requires held-out validation and domain assessment.
  • The workspace is limited to browser-scale one-input regression.

Hard limits that remain

  • The browser is not a substitute for production stiff integration, large sparse algebra, large graph infrastructure, GPU training, structural-identifiability proof, causal inference, or empirical calibration.
  • Optional SciPy verification is a numerical referee and may require a network-loaded Pyodide runtime on first use.
  • Share URLs preserve configuration, not old computed evidence. Recipients must rerun.
  • Representative differential checks are regression evidence, not universal certification.
