Mandelbrot set

Fractals and nature

Mandelbrot, Julia, Barnsley fern, leaves, trees, Sierpinski, Koch snowflakes, dragon curves, chaos games and L-systems show how repeated simple rules generate biological-looking complexity.

Physical and spatial patterns

Chladni-style node sets, simulated Lichtenberg branching, Voronoi cells, Lorenz flow, loxodromes, Apollonian-style packing, Game of Life, Langton’s ant and TSP tours are included as visual mathematics, not research claims.

Curves and symmetry

Butterfly, rhodonea, Lissajous and epicycloid curves expose the visual harmony of periodicity, exponentials and polar geometry.

Number theory

Prime spirals, modular clocks, Collatz paths, continued fractions and toy RSA show the computational play of discrete mathematics.

Topology

Möbius strips and torus sketches give a safe first view of orientability, holes and surface structure without pretending to be a full differential-geometry engine.

Gödel and limits

The logic section is framed as limits of formal systems: axioms, proof rules, self-reference, Gödel numbering, diagonalization and undecidable statements. Not a toy claim to “solve Gödel.”
