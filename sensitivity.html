Which evidence becomes available after each method?

Local: influence ranking, signed sensitivity, trajectory sensitivity, parameter and state Jacobians, parameter × state influence, OFAT, tornado, perturbation convergence, directional response when the direction is feasible, and an optional two-parameter response surface.

Morris: μ*–σ, elementary-effect distributions, convergence, rank stability, normalized parameter-design trajectories, and output paths.

Global variance: first/total indices, optional second-order interactions, first/total effects through time and across model states, variance accounting, sampled relationship matrix, uncertainty, rank stability, convergence, output distribution, optional response surface, and optional limited MI/HSIC screening.

FIM: scaled information matrix, eigenvalue spectrum, and sensitivity-column alignment.

Conditional plots appear only after the corresponding option has been enabled and a fresh result has been computed.

Scientific limits

Computed here: central finite differences; parameter and state Jacobians along the nominal trajectory; trajectory influence maps; OFAT, tornado and range-normalized directional profiles; optional bounded two-parameter response surfaces in Local or Global variance mode; Morris elementary effects; independent-input Jansen first/total indices; optional Saltelli second-order interactions; time-resolved first/total effects and parameter-by-state first/total effects; bootstrap uncertainty; limited histogram-MI and RBF-HSIC permutation screening; and a local finite-difference Fisher-information approximation.

Not computed: adjoint sensitivities, FAST/eFAST, Shapley effects, correlated-input Sobol indices or dependent-input variance decomposition, symbolic identifiability, profile likelihood, certified bifurcation sensitivity, PDE/PINN adjoints or posterior uncertainty. MI and HSIC are limited screening diagnostics; each is not a variance fraction. Requests outside the guarded browser workload envelope are refused before a worker starts.

Rankings are conditional on the chosen output, time window, initial conditions, parameter ranges, perturbation, sample count, solver and tolerances. Initial conditions affect every trajectory but are not varied as sensitivity factors in this release. Close rankings should be treated as unresolved until a larger external analysis confirms them.
